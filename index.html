Methodology & Data Sources
  • PSI (24-hr): https://api.data.gov.sg/v1/environment/psi — National Environment Agency, via data.gov.sg. Official update cadence: every 15 minutes. Historical/nowcast values are pulled from this same endpoint's date-range query, not a separate archive.
  • PM2.5 (1-hr): https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/pm25 — National Environment Agency, via data.gov.sg. Official update cadence: hourly.
  • PSI bands: 0–100 Good to Moderate, 101–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very Unhealthy, 301+ Hazardous — per haze.gov.sg.
  • PM2.5 bands & advisory text: the exact Healthy-person / Vulnerable-person advisory wording in the Personal Advisory panel is copied from haze.gov.sg's own PM2.5 table, not generated. Profiles map to NEA's two published tiers: "General/healthy adult" and "Outdoor worker/exerciser" use the Healthy-person column (with an added note on exertion); Elderly, Pregnant, Young child, and Asthma/chronic condition use the Vulnerable-person column.
  • "Average" figures (live table, nowcast, history chart) are a simple arithmetic mean of the 5 regional readings, computed in this page's own JS — not an NEA-published number.
  • Nowcast compares the current hourly PM2.5 average against readings 1h and 3h earlier (same NEA API, historical query). [Likely] NEA's 24-hr PSI is a rolling average across six pollutants (not just PM2.5) and updates every 15 minutes, but by construction responds more slowly to a sudden change than this hourly PM2.5 trend — this is standard behavior for any rolling-window index; we have not independently verified NEA's internal computation beyond their published update cadence.
  • Staleness: a reading is flagged stale if its own timestamp is older than 2× the source's stated update cadence (30 min for PSI, 2 hr for PM2.5).
  • Map removed: an earlier version showed a region map. NEA publishes only one reference point per region (no finer geographic data is publicly available), so neither a smooth gradient nor a computed boundary shape could be made more accurate than the 5-region table above — it was dropped rather than presented as more precise than it is.
  • Header weather: relative humidity from api.data.gov.sg/v1/environment/relative-humidity (NEA), averaged over however many stations are currently reporting — station coverage on this live endpoint varies and can be as low as 1 station at a given moment, shown as "N st" rather than implied as full-network. Rain likelihood (2h) is NOT an NEA-published probability — NEA publishes no numeric rain probability anywhere we could find. It's a real, computed count: the share of NEA's own ~47 2-hour-forecast areas (.../2-hour-weather-forecast) whose forecast text contains a rain-related word (shower/rain/drizzle/thunder). Hover the figure for the area count and the dominant forecast word.
  • Live-table sort/bars: regions are sorted by PSI descending (worst first). The shaded bar behind each value is proportional to the reading against a fixed display scale (PSI: 0–400, PM2.5: 0–500) and colored by band — a visual aid only, not a separate data source.
  • Min/max scale bars (under the live table) show the 24h and 7d low/high of the island-wide average against a fixed band-colored scale (PSI 0–400, PM2.5 0–500), with a marker for the current reading — computed from the same hourly series as the trend charts below.
  • 7-Day and 24-Hour Trend charts: both show PSI and PM2.5 as continuous lines. Each chart's x-axis is a full fixed hourly grid for its declared window (168 points for 7 days, 24 for 1 day) built independently of what data actually arrived — a day or hour that failed to load is a visible gap in the line (not interpolated), and the axis always spans the complete window rather than shrinking to whatever arrived.
  • Momentum badge (top-right of Live Readings): mean island-wide average PSI over the last 24h vs the preceding 24h. Any increase shows red (worsening), any decrease shows green (improving) — not banded into a "steady" dead zone.
  • Region divergence flag (⚠): shown when a region's PSI is more than 25 points above the island average — a chosen threshold meant to surface a possible localized smoke plume early, not an NEA-defined alert.
  • Wind panel: current conditions are a circular mean of NEA's live wind-direction and wind-speed station readings (.../wind-direction, .../wind-speed) — direction shown is where the wind is blowing FROM. The 4-day forecast (direction, speed range, general outlook) is NEA/MSS's own published .../4-day-weather-forecast API, not derived. A ⚠ appears when a direction falls in the W–SW quadrant, the general direction of Sumatra fire activity toward Singapore — background context from general knowledge of regional haze patterns, not a verified source-attribution model.
  • Nowcast "24h ago": compares the current hourly reading to the same hour yesterday, using the same NEA date-range query as the live panel and trend charts.
  • No probabilistic forecast model (Monte Carlo baseline, backtest, Tier 2 commentary ingestion) has been built yet. Yahoo Weather AQI and IQAir data were investigated but not added — Yahoo's public API was retired in 2019 with no confirmed AQI field on its successor, and IQAir requires a user-created API key that would be exposed client-side on this static site.
