Methodology & Data Sources
  • PSI (24-hr): https://api.data.gov.sg/v1/environment/psi — National Environment Agency, via data.gov.sg. Official update cadence: every 15 minutes. Historical values on this page are pulled from the same endpoint's date-range query, not a separate archive.
  • PM2.5 (1-hr): https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/pm25 — National Environment Agency, via data.gov.sg. Official update cadence: hourly. Historical values likewise pulled from this same endpoint.
  • PSI bands: 0–100 Good to Moderate, 101–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very Unhealthy, 301+ Hazardous — per haze.gov.sg (NEA's official haze microsite).
  • PM2.5 bands: 0–55 Band 1 (Normal), 56–150 Band 2 (Elevated), 151–250 Band 3 (High), 251+ Band 4 (Very High) — per haze.gov.sg.
  • "Island-wide" figures (live panel average, and the historical chart lines) are a simple arithmetic mean of the 5 regional readings, computed in this page's own JS — not an NEA-published number.
  • Map region shapes are a Voronoi partition of NEA's 5 exact reference points, clipped to Singapore's landmass outline (from URA's Master Plan 2019 Region Boundary dataset, used here only for its coastline — its own internal region split is a different scheme than NEA's and is not used). Computed offline by scripts/build_region_voronoi.py, not an NEA-published boundary — see the disclaimer under the map. Base map tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Staleness: a live-panel reading is flagged stale if its own timestamp (not the fetch time) is older than 2× the source's stated update cadence (30 min for PSI, 2 hr for PM2.5).
  • No forecast model (Monte Carlo baseline, backtest, Tier 2 commentary ingestion) has been built yet. This page is live + historical readings only.
